Our Craft Standard

Baked with reverence for real bread.

No shortcuts, no artificial dough improvers, and no industrial yeast speed-ups. Here is how we earn your breakfast table.

36h Cold Fermentation

Extended slow ferment breaks down complex gluten bonds for extraordinary honeycomb crumb texture and easy, gentle digestion.

Stone-Milled Flours

We source non-GMO heritage rye, whole wheat, and spelt directly from independent growers, milled unbleached with germ intact.

Pure Normandy Butter

Our viennoiserie pastry sheets feature 84% fat European cultured butter for deeply caramelized, gossamer pastry layers.

Bespoke Celebrations

From modern botanical tiered wedding cakes to bespoke birthdays, every celebration design is sculpted with botanical elegance.
